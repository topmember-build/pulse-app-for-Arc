      Questions people ask.

      Why a heart?

      The heart is Pulse's visual metaphor for onchain activity. Token results list the live Transfer events and exact recent block range they analyze; they do not assume every transfer went through a known liquidity pool.

      Do scans cost anything?

      No. Token and contract scans are free during the Arc testnet beta. Payment settlement is not enabled, and the app will not request a deposit or scan payment. Pulse never asks for a seed phrase.

      Which chain does it support?

      Arc only. Staying on one chain lets us label Arc's contracts and launchpads properly instead of guessing.

      Is this finished?

      This is an Arc testnet beta with Privy wallet connection and live RPC reads. Token analysis covers only the latest 500 blocks; contract scans currently check basic runtime facts. Payment settlement and deeper security analysis are not enabled.
