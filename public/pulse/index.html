      Questions people ask.

      Why a heart?

      On Arc, a token's money sits in a pool that every buy and sell passes through, much like blood through a heart. Showing it that way means the picture and the data are the same thing: it beats once per block, money in is blue and money out is red.

      How do I pay, and does Pulse hold my money?

      Scanning is a payment, so you connect first. You can connect a wallet you already have, or continue with email and Privy creates an embedded wallet. You deposit a small USDC balance once, and each scan takes 0.001 from it. With your own wallet your keys stay with you and you approve scans yourself. A Privy embedded wallet is meant for scan credit rather than savings, so keep the balance small. Pulse never asks for a seed phrase, and looking at results moves no money.

      Which chain does it support?

      Arc only. Staying on one chain lets us label Arc's contracts and launchpads properly instead of guessing.

      Is this finished?

      Pulse is being wired for Arc testnet with live Privy wallet connection and onchain pay-per-call settlement. Findings still explain what the scanner can and cannot prove.
